# 06. Jira Administration - Users and Sites

Admin work in Jira Cloud happens in two places:

| Place | URL | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Atlassian Administration** | https://admin.atlassian.com | Users, groups, products, sites, security, billing |
| **Jira settings** (gear icon inside Jira) | `https://your-domain.atlassian.net` | Projects, issue types, workflows, fields |

You need the **organization admin** or **site admin** role to see these pages.

## Sites and Products

A **site** is one Atlassian address, such as `your-domain.atlassian.net`. Many companies keep two:

| Site | Example | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Production | `cloudops.atlassian.net` | Real work |
| Development | `devcloudops.atlassian.net` | Testing configuration and scripts safely |

To add a product (for example Jira Service Management or Confluence) to a site:

1. Open https://admin.atlassian.com and choose your organization.
2. Go to **Products** (sometimes under *Apps*) and choose **Add product**.
3. Pick the product, then the **site** it should be added to.

> Test new workflows, automation rules and Python scripts on the development site first. A script that creates 20 tickets on production is hard to explain.

## Managing Users

In **Atlassian Administration > Directory > Users**:

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| **Invite** a user | **Invite users**, enter emails, choose the products and role, then send |
| **Give product access** | Open the user, then **Add product access** (or edit the product roles) |
| **Suspend** access | Open the user, then **Suspend access**. They can't sign in, but their issues and history stay |
| **Remove** a user | Removes them from the site. Use it for accounts that should be cleaned up completely |
| **Groups** | **Directory > Groups**. Give permissions to groups instead of individuals |

### User Invite Settings

In **Products > User access settings** (or **Directory > User invites**) you control who can join:

- Allow **anyone with an approved email domain** (for example `@cloudops.com`) to join automatically.
- Require **admin approval** for every invite request.
- Allow existing users to **invite** colleagues.

> **Good practice:** when someone leaves the company, **suspend** their account first. Reassign their open issues with a JQL search like `assignee = "Former Employee" AND resolution = Unresolved`, then remove the account.
