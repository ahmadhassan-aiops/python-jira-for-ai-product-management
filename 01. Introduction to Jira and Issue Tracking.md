# 01. Introduction to Jira and Issue Tracking

**Jira** is a work management tool from Atlassian. Teams use it to **track** every piece of work, from a customer complaint to a new feature, from the moment it's reported until it's resolved.

Official site: https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira

## Why Teams Need Issue Tracking

Imagine customers report: *"We can't open our course page on the website."*

Without a tracking tool, the report lives in an email or a chat message. Nobody knows who owns it, what has been tried, or how long it has been open.

With Jira, the same report becomes a **ticket** (an *issue*, called a *work item* in newer Jira Cloud sites) with a clear history:

| Day | What happens in the ticket |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | A support agent **creates** the ticket and assigns it to a developer, Bilal |
| Day 1 | Bilal adds a **comment**: working on the page issue, blocked by a network problem. He **logs 7 hours** of work |
| Day 2 | Bilal **updates** the ticket: still getting an error while resolving |
| Day 3 | No update. The team lead sees the ticket is 3 days old and follows up |
| Day 4 | Bilal fixes the bug and moves the ticket to **Resolved** |

Anyone (the manager, the customer success team, the next on-call engineer) can open the ticket and see exactly where things stand.

## What Jira Tracks

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Issue / work item** | One unit of work: a bug, a task, a story, a request |
| **Project** | A container of related issues, for example *Website* or *IT Infrastructure* |
| **Assignee** | The person responsible for the issue right now |
| **Reporter** | The person who raised it |
| **Status** | Where the issue is in its workflow: *To Do*, *In Progress*, *Done* |
| **Priority** | How urgent it is: *Highest* to *Lowest* |
| **Comments** | The conversation about the issue |
| **Worklog** | Time spent on the issue |

## Where Jira Fits for AI and Product Teams

- **Product managers** plan releases, write user stories and prioritise the backlog.
- **Engineering and ML teams** track experiments, model bugs, data pipeline failures and deployment tasks.
- **Operations and support teams** turn incidents and customer requests into tickets with response targets.
- **Automation** (the second half of this course) connects Jira to Python, so monitoring scripts can open tickets and send updates without a human.

## Jira Products

| Product | Built for |
|---|---|
| **Jira** (formerly Jira Software) | Software, product and engineering teams: Scrum and Kanban boards, sprints, backlogs |
| **Jira Service Management** | IT and support teams: service desks, SLAs, customer portals |
| **Jira Product Discovery** | Product managers: collecting ideas and prioritising what to build |
